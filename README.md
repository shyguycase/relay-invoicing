# Relay

## Turn client conversations into money you actually collect.

Relay is a smart invoicing assistant for freelancers and solo consultants. It extracts financial agreements from messy emails and Slack messages, tracks unbilled work in a beautiful dashboard, and drafts professional follow-up emails automatically.

### Key Features
*   **Hybrid AI Engine:** Defaults to your local machine for total privacy and zero cost, but seamlessly falls back to the cloud if your local server is off.
*   **One-Click Follow-Ups:** Automatically drafts polite, professional emails to chase overdue invoices.
*   **Professional Invoices:** Generate clean, print-ready PDF invoices instantly.
*   **100% Private:** Your client data stays on your machine. No complex CRM setups or monthly subscriptions.
