# WELCOME TO RELAY

Thank you for purchasing Relay. 

## HOW TO START
1. Double-click the `Relay.exe` file to launch the application.
2. The app will open automatically in a clean, dedicated window. 

## HOW THE AI WORKS
Relay uses a hybrid AI engine to ensure you never wait. 
- **Cloud Fallback:** For the fastest, most reliable extraction, Relay seamlessly uses its secure cloud fallback. 
- **Local & Private:** You can also connect your local LM Studio server in the Settings menu for 100% private, offline processing at zero cost.

## HOW TO USE
- **Add:** Click "Add obligation" in the top right and paste an email or Slack message. The AI will instantly extract the client, scope, and price into your dashboard.
- **Invoice:** Click "Invoice" next to any item to open a clean, print-ready PDF.
- **Follow-Up:** Click "Draft follow-up" to instantly generate a polite, professional payment reminder you can copy and send.

*Your data is stored securely on your local machine. No complex CRM setups. No monthly subscriptions.*
